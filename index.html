How to use

  • Side View (recommended): place your phone to the side so your whole body profile is visible. Best for checking squat depth and forward lean.
  • Front View: place your phone facing you. Best for checking if your knees cave inward.
  • Stand ~1.5–2.5m from the camera, in good lighting, with your full body in frame.
  • This app runs entirely in your browser — no video is uploaded anywhere.
  • This is a form guide, not medical advice. Stop if anything hurts.
